# injera

A blank notebook. The 2011 archive has a copy of someone else's recipe for this; it stays in `.planning/old-recipes-and-notes/` as reference, and this page is where a version of our own gets worked out.

## reference

- **Archived copy:** `injera.md` (2011-07): Michigan State University Extension, [Injera](https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/injera).
- **What that version leans on** (summarized, not quoted):
  - Teff cut with all-purpose flour (about 1:3 by volume), water to a thin batter
  - Fermented on the counter for one to three days until bubbly and sour, with a pinch of yeast as a fallback
  - Poured thin into a hot pan, cooked on one side only until the surface is covered in holes and dry
