A blank notebook. The 2011 archive has a copy of someone else's recipe for this; it stays in `.planning/old-recipes-and-notes/` as reference, and this page is where a version of our own gets worked out.

## reference

- **Archived copy:** `street-tacos.md` (2011-04): J. Kenji López-Alt, "No-Waste Tacos de Carnitas With Salsa Verde", Serious Eats.
- **What that version leans on** (summarized, not quoted):
  - Pork shoulder cubes packed tight in a baking dish with orange (juice and the spent halves), onion, garlic, bay, cinnamon, and a little oil; covered, low oven, around 3½ hours, so it confits in its own rendered fat with no added lard
  - The drained liquid becomes the base for a tomatillo–jalapeño salsa verde; the fat goes back onto the pork
  - Crisped under the broiler in two rounds just before serving
