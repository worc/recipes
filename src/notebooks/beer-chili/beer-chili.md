## reference

- **Archived copy:** `beer-chili.md` (2011-09): Imbibe Magazine's [Guinness Chili](https://imbibemagazine.com/recipe/guinness-chili-recipe/). The live page has diced tomatoes where the archived copy has tomato sauce.
- **What that version leans on** (summarized, not quoted):
  - Bacon rendered first; the beef, onion, and garlic brown in its fat
  - Stout used twice: once to deglaze, once as the simmering liquid
  - Three kinds of canned beans with their liquid, a heavy hand of chili powder plus cumin and cayenne, then 2–3 hours low and covered
  - A warning that salt concentrates in the leftovers
