## reference

- **What that version leans on** (summarized, not quoted):
  - Onion and garlic sweated in oil, then broth, canned black beans, canned tomatoes with green chiles, chili powder, cumin
  - A short simmer (minutes, not hours), cilantro and lime off the heat
  - Blended smooth, toppings do the rest
