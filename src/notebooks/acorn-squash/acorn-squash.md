# acorn squash

A blank notebook. The 2011 archive has a copy of someone else's recipe for this; it stays in `.planning/old-recipes-and-notes/` as reference, and this page is where a version of our own gets worked out.

## reference

- **Archived copy:** `acorn-squash.md` (2011-11; live on the site 2023–2026). The same text appears at [newengland.com](https://newengland.com/food/acorn-squash/); it may be syndicated from somewhere older, so provenance is uncertain.
- **What that version leans on** (summarized, not quoted):
  - Halved stem to tip, seeded, flesh scored, baked cut side up with a little water in the pan so the skin doesn't scorch
  - Butter, brown sugar, and maple syrup in each cavity
  - Hot oven (400°F), about an hour or more, until very soft and browned; baste with whatever syrup pools
